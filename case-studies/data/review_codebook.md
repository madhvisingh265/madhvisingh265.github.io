# Review codebook (Google Play, pulled 24 Nov 2026)

Source: the "most relevant" review list for each app on Google Play (India, English), scrolled to load ~400 reviews per app.
Reviewer names were not collected. Counts below are for 1–2 star reviews. A review can match more than one theme.

| Theme | Matches (case-insensitive regex) |
|---|---|
| paid_push | premium, paid, subscri…, fast forward, payment, charg…, money back, refund |
| scam_fake | scam, fake, fraud, cheat, registration fee, asking (for) money |
| no_response | no call/response/reply/interview, not a single call/interview/response, never got/heard, ghost, zero call/response, didn't get any |
| irrelevant | irrelevant, not relevant, unrelated, wrong job/role, not matching, mismatch |
| bugs_ux | bug, crash, OTP, login, slow, not working, glitch, hang, error |
| stale_listings | expired, old job, outdated, already closed/filled, no longer, inactive |
| fresher_exp | fresher, experience required, entry level, no experience, years of experience |
| spam_contact | spam, unwanted call/message, WhatsApp, Telegram, too many calls/mails/notifications |

Limits: keyword matching misses sarcasm and other languages; "most relevant" is Google's ordering, not a random sample;
Naukri's sample is almost all 2026, while Internshala and Unstop span 2018–2026; not every reviewer is a fresher.
